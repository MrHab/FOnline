# Free armor replacement sources

The shipped armor replacements are derived only from freely licensed models. Source downloads are reproducible with `node tools/fetch-free-armor-sources.js`; the downloader pins every file by SHA-256 and stores downloads under the ignored `Build/SourceDownloads` directory.

## Modifications

All source geometry was isolated from the original character, fitted to the six Realm of Ashes body types, rebound to the current 65-bone player rig, cropped where needed, assigned existing Realm of Ashes materials, and constrained to a maximum of 12,000 triangles per armor variant. The generated review reports retain the creator, source page, license, source checksum, and optimization data for every variant.
